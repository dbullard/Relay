# Community conduct

Relay welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive conversations, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and made in good faith.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer at support@ondeckapps.com. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. The project's maintainers will review concerns as their availability permits; no response-time commitment is implied.
